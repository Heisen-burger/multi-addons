Fuel Price Telegram Bot

Fuel prices around you on Telegram: share your location, pick a fuel, get the cheapest stations nearby with a navigation link. Follow one or more fuels of a station and receive an alert at every change, or only outside the thresholds you set.

Features

  • Location: nearest stations for the chosen fuel, cheapest first, with distance, last communication time and a 🧭 Navigate link; toggles for distance order, self/served and fuel.
  • Search by town or station name.
  • Station card with a venue pin and one button per fuel: tap to follow, tap again to stop.
  • Previous price: every price the bot sends shows the move against the price before it.
  • History: a button on the station card, or /storico, lists the last changes of that station.
  • Thresholds: above X, below Y, outside a range, or every change.
  • Alerts sent by the observatory sync, no extra cron.
  • /lista to manage subscriptions, /prezzi for their current prices.

Configuration

  1. Token from @BotFather in Settings > Telegram.
  2. Fuel Prices > Configuration > Register Telegram Webhook.
